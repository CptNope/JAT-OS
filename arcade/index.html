How to play

Most games accept keyboard, touch, or S Pen taps. Built-in games also support standard controllers when the browser exposes the Gamepad API.

Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move; A to select, start, or rotate; B to go back or restart in supported games. Click or tap inside the game first if the controller is not responding.

Fullscreen: use the game toolbar. Press Escape to return when the browser is in fullscreen.

Your local score history isn't shared with WordPress accounts or other devices.
