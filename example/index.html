Switch to JAT OS

JAT OS turns this page into a desktop: windows you can drag, snap and resize, a launcher, notifications and settings. The website stays exactly as it is underneath.

  1. Press Enter JAT OS, at the bottom right of every page.
  2. Open apps from the dock, or press Ctrl + Space to search.
  3. Choose Dock, Taskbar, Shelf or Activities in Settings.
  4. Choose Exit to website, or press your browser’s Back button, to return here.
